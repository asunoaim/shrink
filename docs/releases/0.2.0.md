## shrink 0.2.0: the playhead got a gym membership

Real talk: the old playhead had the grip strength of a wet bar of soap. You tried to grab it, it said "no", and you missed your flick. That era is over.

### the playhead glow-up 💪
- **Big chunky knob** with a fat invisible grab zone. You can't miss it anymore, even on a 3 AM caffeine shake.
- **Drag anywhere on the time bar** above the thumbnails.
- **Hover preview:** a ghost line shows the exact time under your mouse, so you know where you land before you click.
- **Mouse wheel skips 1 second** (Shift = 5 s). Scroll-to-scrub, like a civilized person.

### highlights, but smoother
- **Click a highlight** (or hit **Tab** / **Shift+Tab**) and the playhead jumps to its start. Hit Space and it plays, **and it keeps playing past the end** so you can see what comes after. Avidemux users, you're home now.
- Every clip now opens in **Original** mode. No more surprise Shrink mode because you used it once three days ago.

### shrink respects your frames 🎯
When your target is too small, the one-click fix now **keeps your fps and drops resolution first**: 1440p120 → 1080p120 → 720p120. Only if 720p at full fps still looks like soup does it offer 60 fps. 120 fps gang stays 120 fps gang.

### small fixes nobody will notice but me
- Space and the arrow keys work while exporting. Watch your clip while it cooks.
- The "too small" warning no longer flickers when you yank the slider around.

If you're reading this in the update popup: you clicked "Update & restart" and it just worked. Look at you. 🫡
