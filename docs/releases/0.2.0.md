## shrink 0.2.0: you can grab the playhead now

In 0.1.0 the playhead was a 2-pixel line and grabbing it was a skill check. Most people failed. I failed. Skill issue (mine). Fixed.

- The playhead has a big knob now, and you can grab it from a bit to the side too.
- Drag along the time bar to scrub.
- Hover over the timeline to see the exact time before you click.
- Mouse wheel skips 1 second (Shift: 5).
- Click a highlight or press Tab to jump to its start. Space plays it and keeps going after the end.
- Every clip opens in Original mode now. Shrink mode stopped showing up uninvited.
- Size fixes keep your frame rate: resolution drops first (down to 720p), frame rate only after that. Frames are sacred.
- Space and the arrow keys work while exporting.

Found something broken? It's a feature until you [open an issue](https://github.com/asunoaim/shrink/issues). 🫡
