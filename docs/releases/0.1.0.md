## shrink 0.1.0: it exists now

Getting one clip ready used to take two apps: one to cut it, one to make it small enough to send. Sometimes a third, because the second one was being weird. That's a lot of apps for 20 seconds of gameplay, fr. So I made one app that does both. It's called shrink. It shrinks. Naming is hard.

- Mark highlights on the timeline (drag, or press I and O). Each one becomes its own file.
- **Original:** cuts without re-encoding. Instant, no quality lost.
- **Shrink:** pick a size, get the best quality that fits. Never goes over. I tested this more than I'd like to admit.
- Size too small to look good? It warns you and offers a fix. It won't judge you. Much.
- Uses your graphics card if it can, your CPU if it has to.
- Finished files land in your clipboard. Paste them straight into your group chat.
- Right-click a video → *Show more options* → **Open in shrink**.
- Updates itself, so you only download it once.

**Installing:** download `shrink_0.1.0_x64-setup.exe` below. Windows will say *"Windows protected your PC"* because I can't afford a code-signing certificate yet. Click **More info → Run anyway**. The code is all here if you want to check.

Bugs or ideas → [open an issue](https://github.com/asunoaim/shrink/issues). Response time: eventually™.
