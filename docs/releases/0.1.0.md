## shrink 0.1.0: one tool. that's it. that's the release.

**POV:** you just hit a disgusting 1v4. Your hands are still shaking. Now the *real* boss fight starts:

1. open Avidemux to cut the 20 seconds that matter
2. open HandBrake (or a cursed 3 AM PowerShell script) to get it under Discord's upload limit
3. upload it, and the embed won't even play 💀

Three tools for one clip. In this economy? Nah. So I built **shrink**: cut it, shrink it, paste it. Done.

### what it does
- ✂️ **Mark as many highlights as you want.** Drag on the timeline or smash **I** / **O**. Every highlight becomes its own file.
- 🎞️ **Original mode:** lossless and instant, straight into your montage folder. No re-encode, no quality tax.
- 🗜️ **Shrink mode:** pick a size like 10 MB and get the best quality that fits. **It never goes over.** Not even by one byte. Pinky promise, it's tested.
- 🟨 Target too small? It tells you before you export and offers a one-click fix, not a surprise potato.
- 🚀 Uses your NVIDIA / AMD / Intel GPU. CPU gang is supported too, just slower.
- 📋 Finished files go straight to your clipboard. Ctrl+V into Discord and farm those reactions.
- 🖱️ Right-click any video → *Show more options* → **Open in shrink**.
- 🔄 Updates itself from here on, so you only download this once.

### installing
Grab `shrink_0.1.0_x64-setup.exe` below. Windows will panic with *"Windows protected your PC"* because I don't have a $300 code-signing certificate (yet). Click **More info → Run anyway**. It's fine, it's open source, read every line if you want.

Bugs, ideas, hot takes → [issues](https://github.com/asunoaim/shrink/issues). Go touch grass after exporting. Or don't, I'm not your mom.
