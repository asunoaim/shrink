The first release of shrink: a small Windows app that cuts highlights out of your gameplay clips and shrinks them to a size you pick.

**What it does**
- Mark as many highlights as you like on the timeline (drag, or press I and O). Each one becomes its own file.
- **Original**: lossless and instant, for montages.
- **Shrink**: re-encodes to a size you pick (like 10 MB for Discord) and never goes over it. If the size is too small for good quality, it warns you and offers a one-click fix.
- Uses your NVIDIA, AMD or Intel graphics card, or the CPU if there isn't one.
- Finished files are copied to the clipboard, so Ctrl+V in Discord just works.
- Right-click any video → *Show more options* → **Open in shrink**.
- Checks for updates by itself from now on.

**Installing**
Download `shrink_0.1.0_x64-setup.exe` below and run it. Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't signed with a paid certificate yet. Click **More info → Run anyway**.
