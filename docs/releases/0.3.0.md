## shrink 0.3.0: Tab got its old job back

Tab used to jump between highlights, which was rude, because Tab already had a job: moving between buttons. Highlights have their own keys now and Tab is back at work. Character development.

- **Q/E** jump between highlights (Q back, E forward), so your left hand never has to leave the keyboard.
- Clicking a highlight puts the playhead exactly where you clicked — originally a bug, now a feature with paperwork.
- Tab moves between buttons again and shows you where you are (sorry, keyboard people).
- **⚙ Settings** exist now: where clips get saved (ask / next to the recording / one folder forever), clipboard on or off, and what every clip starts with. No more picking the same folder 40 times a day.
- Clips open faster: the video shows up right away, thumbnails catch up a moment later.
- Buttons got dark text, because white on orange looked cool and read terribly.
- Labels say what they do ("Original quality", "Shrink to size", "Audio 1"), and "Track1" has been retired with honors.
- Errors come as one plain sentence, with the scary part tucked behind a **Details** button.

Found something broken? It's a feature until you [open an issue](https://github.com/asunoaim/shrink/issues). 🫡
